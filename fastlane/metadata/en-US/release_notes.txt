Version 1.0 is the first release of Gallipot.

Scan a grocery tin, set its best before, and land it on Use Soon. Pull the facing pack so the older date is used first. A newer pack stays behind until that one is gone.

Pantry shows stacked dates behind each tin. Past dates fold to Spent at midnight and can be culled.

Settings includes How Pull works, a tappable Open Food Facts credit, Contact Gallipot, Export CSV, Replay walkthrough, and Reset all data.

No account, no in-app purchase.
