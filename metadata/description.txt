Gallipot is a pantry log for households that waste food because a newer pack sits in front of an older one. Scan a grocery tin, set its best before, and pull the older pack first.

• Use Soon shows the facing tin and days left. Pull peels that pack. A newer pack stays behind until the older one is gone.
• Scan a tin with the camera, pick a sample code, type a code, or search a tin name. Set Best before and QTY, then Land.
• Pantry lists stacked dates behind each tin. FACE is the soonest live date. Spent dates wait for Cull.
• Undo peels the last pull or the last tin.
• Settings includes How Pull works, Open Food Facts, Contact Gallipot, Export CSV, Replay walkthrough, and Reset all data.

No account is required. Tins, dates, and pulls stay on this device.
